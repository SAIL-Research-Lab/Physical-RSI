# Physical RSI Paper Reference Index

This index mirrors the bibliography of **Physical Recursive Self-Improvement**. The PDF contains
105 bibliography records. Three works appear twice as preprint and publication records: AutoRT,
Robometer, and Darwin Godel Machine. Those pairs are consolidated below, leaving **102 unique
works**.

The index is alphabetical by first author, as in the paper. Placement here means that a work is
cited by the paper; it does not mean that the work demonstrates Physical RSI or a particular L1-L5
capability. The evidence claims in the [repository overview](../README.md) remain the authoritative
guide to that distinction.
